Core Lending

Major & Registered Banks (Tier 1)

The cornerstone of New Zealand lending. Registered banks offer competitive carded interest rates, fixed-term structures, and cater to standard mortgage applications (PAYE income, 20%+ deposit, strong credit). However, their policies can be rigid, meaning if you fall outside their standard criteria, an approved non-bank partner may be recommended.
