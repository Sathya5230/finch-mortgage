Industrial and Commercial Bank of China (NZ) — NZ Mortgage Review (2026)

Industrial and Commercial Bank of China (NZ) is global Chinese bank with NZ commercial and residential lending capability. For New Zealand borrowers, the lender's specialties include commercial property lending, residential lending for offshore-linked clients. As an independent NZ mortgage broker, Finch arranges loans through ICBC NZ alongside the full panel of registered NZ banks (ANZ, ASB, BNZ, Westpac, Kiwibank, TSB, SBS, The Co-operative Bank, Heartland) and the specialist non-bank market — so we can recommend ICBC NZ only when their offer genuinely beats the alternatives for your scenario.

Who ICBC NZ Suits Best

ICBC NZ suits borrowers with offshore income or links to the Chinese banking system. We see strongest outcomes when the client's income profile, deposit position, and intended property align with ICBC NZ's current scorecard. Outside those scenarios, another NZ lender will usually price sharper or move faster — which is why we compare every option before recommending.

ICBC NZ — Strengths

  • Specialist in offshore-linked client lending
  • Strong commercial property lending

ICBC NZ — Considerations

  • Smaller residential mortgage book
  • Limited consumer brand presence in NZ

How ICBC NZ Compares Across the NZ Lender Panel

No single NZ lender wins for every scenario. Pricing varies by week and by deal type — ICBC NZ may be sharpest one month and uncompetitive the next, while a non-bank like Resimac or Pepper Money picks up cases the main banks decline. Use our live NZ rates comparison to see where ICBC NZ sits today, then book a free consultation to match your scenario against the full panel.

Documents ICBC NZ Typically Requires

  • 3 months payslips (PAYE) or 2 years accountant-signed financials (self-employed)
  • 3 months bank statements across every account including credit cards
  • KiwiSaver provider statement and annual summary (for first home buyers)
  • NZ photo ID + proof of address (AML compliance)
  • Evidence of deposit and recent savings history

Service Coverage Across NZ

Finch arranges ICBC NZ lending for clients across the country — Auckland, Wellington, Christchurch, Hamilton, Tauranga, Dunedin, Palmerston North, Napier, Nelson, Queenstown, and regional NZ. We submit your file via the lender's broker channel which usually delivers a faster, sharper outcome than walking into a branch.

Should You Go Direct, or Use a Broker?

Going direct to ICBC NZ only gives you ICBC NZ. Using Finch costs you nothing — ICBC NZ (where they're the right answer) pays the broker fee on settlement, not you — and you get the comparison across every other NZ lender for free. Read our real NZ client case studies for examples.
