Credit Unions & Customer-Owned

Unlike corporate banks which prioritize profits for external shareholders, Credit Unions and mutual financial institutions are 100% owned by their members. They reinvest profits to offer competitive rates and lower fees. They offer a highly personalized, human approach to underwriting.
