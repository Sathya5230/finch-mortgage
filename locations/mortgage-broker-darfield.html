Local knowledge

Buying and refinancing in Darfield

Darfield is the main town of the Malvern area of Selwyn, on the road to Arthur's Pass. The magnitude 7.1 earthquake of 4 September 2010 was centred nearby, the start of the Canterbury earthquake sequence.

Today it's a growing town for families and commuters to Rolleston and Christchurch, surrounded by lifestyle blocks. Earthquake history, lifestyle-block rules and new-build lending are the main themes.

What lenders look at in Darfield

The local details most likely to decide which lender says yes.

Earthquake risk and building checks

The September 2010 Canterbury earthquake was centred near Darfield, beginning the earthquake sequence that later devastated Christchurch. Lenders need full replacement insurance, and unrepaired damage or open claims can hold up an approval.

Lifestyle blocks and rural-residential land

Plenty of Darfield buyers are looking at land around Kirwee, Sheffield and the Malvern Hills. Lenders treat a block as residential only up to a set land area and use, and look harder at farm income, tank water and septic systems, so the right lender matters.

Commuting from Darfield

Many Darfield households commute to Rolleston and Christchurch. Lenders assess the whole household budget, travel costs included, rather than pricing the commute itself.

New subdivisions and new builds

New housing is going up on the edges of town as Darfield grows. New builds are exempt from the Reserve Bank's LVR restrictions, so some lenders accept a smaller deposit, but the bank values the home at completion.

Refinancing your Darfield home

  • A refinance valuation may pick up unrepaired earthquake damage, so settle any open claims before you switch.
  • Refinancing a lifestyle block means a fresh valuation, and the new lender's own land-size and water rules apply, so we check those before you apply.

The usual checks still apply: your fixed-term end date, any break fee and any cashback clawback. The NZ refinance guide walks through them, and the refinance savings calculator shows what a lower rate is worth.
