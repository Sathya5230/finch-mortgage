With auctions and quick tenders dominating the Napier & Hawke's Bay sales landscape, a pre-approval is your ticket to participate. Eligibility & Mortgage Process

To qualify for a pre-approval, you must submit a complete financial profile demonstrating servicing capacity and a verified deposit source (cash, KiwiSaver, or equity). Pre-approvals are generally valid for 90 days, allowing you to bid at auction or make unconditional offers. The process involves organizing your checklist, matching your files to the right bank's scorecard, and securing the approval letter. We will review any property you want to offer on to ensure the bank accepts that specific property type.

Why Pre-Approval is Critical in Napier & Hawke's Bay

In New Zealand, many properties are sold via auction or deadline sale. If you bid at auction, your bid is legally binding and completely unconditional. Without pre-approval, bidding is extremely risky:

  • Auction Ready: A pre-approval allows you to complete due diligence on the property (such as building reports and title checks) and bid unconditionally on auction day.
  • Negotiation Power: Sellers prefer cash buyers or unconditional offers. A pre-approved buyer can negotiate a lower purchase price by offering a quick, hassle-free settlement.
  • Budget Security: Knowing your exact spending limit stops you from falling in love with properties that exceed your bank's borrowing criteria.

Common Conditions on Pre-Approvals

A pre-approval is rarely unconditional. Lenders issue them with a list of conditions that must be met before they release the funds. We work with you to clear these conditions systematically:

  • Registered Valuation: Lenders may require a valuation from an approved panel (like Valocity or CoreLogic) to confirm the property matches the purchase price.
  • Building Report: For older properties, plaster finishes, or suspected weather-tightness issues, banks require a comprehensive invasive or non-invasive moisture test report.
  • KiwiSaver Withdrawal Confirmation: Proof that your KiwiSaver funds have been approved for release by your provider.
  • Clean Title Review: The bank's legal department must review the title (e.g. fee simple, cross-lease, or unit title) to ensure no restrictive easements exist.

How to Maintain Your Pre-Approval Status

Once your pre-approval is issued, it is valid for 90 days. However, the bank can revoke it if your financial situation changes. To protect your approval, follow these guidelines:

  1. Do Not Take on New Debt: Avoid buying new cars, opening store credit accounts, or signing up for interest-free hire purchases.
  2. Maintain Your Current Employment: Do not change jobs, resign to start a business, or transition to a commission-only role without consulting us first.
  3. Protect Your Deposit: Keep your deposit funds safe in a savings account. Do not spend portion of your deposit or invest it in high-risk assets.
