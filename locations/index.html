Finch Service Locations

Independent, $0-fee mortgage advice blended with local market insights. Choose your service and city combination below to view detailed criteria, price bands, and local processes.

Lending policy is not applied identically everywhere in New Zealand. Property values, rental yields, and the mix of first-home buyers versus investors differ from city to city, and individual banks adjust their appetite for certain property types and locations accordingly. A broker who understands these regional differences can steer you toward the lenders most likely to say yes in your specific market.

Each location and service combination below reflects the criteria, price bands, and processes relevant to that city, so you can see what applies to your situation before booking a call rather than starting from a generic national overview.
