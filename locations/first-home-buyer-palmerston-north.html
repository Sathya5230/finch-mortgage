What first home buyers in Palmerston North are up against

Deposit requirements, LVR exemptions and First Home Loan eligibility all play out differently by region. Here is what matters in Palmerston North.

Defence postings at Linton and Ohakea

Linton Military Camp and RNZAF Base Ohakea bring many defence families to the Manawatū. Postings can mean buying now and renting the home out later. We structure the loan so it still works if you're posted elsewhere and the home becomes a rental.

Affordable entry for first home buyers

Palmerston North has been one of the more affordable cities, so first home buyers can often combine KiwiSaver with the Kāinga Ora First Home Loan where eligible. We check eligibility and which participating lender suits you.

Logistics and distribution jobs

The city is a major distribution hub, with many shift workers in warehousing and freight. Lenders treat overtime and allowances differently. We gather the right income history so your real pay is counted.

Older weatherboard housing

Many Palmerston North homes are older weatherboard builds that need insulation, heating or re-roofing. Some lenders will fund those improvements as part of the loan. We factor a builder's report into the plan.

Palmerston North at a glance

Typical Palmerston North price bands

Indicatively, Palmerston North standalone homes typically $550k-$850k, Hokowhitu and Aokautere $700k-$1m, Feilding $550k-$800k, Manawatū lifestyle blocks $850k-$1.5m. Indicative ranges only, not a valuation.

Who we typically help in Palmerston North

We regularly arrange finance for first home buyers across Palmerston North suburbs; NZDF and university employees; investors targeting student-rental yield near Massey; lifestyle-block buyers across the Manawatū.
