## Internal Linking Best Practices

1. **Link naturally**: Only link when genuinely relevant and helpful to the reader
2. **Prefer specific over hub pages**: Link to the specific lender review or location page, not just `/lenders.html` or the city hub, when a specific match exists
3. **3-5 links per post**: Standard blog posts should carry 3-5 internal links
4. **Cluster-first linking**: Link within the same topic cluster before cross-linking unrelated clusters — avoids the cannibalization risk already visible between e.g. `how-much-can-i-borrow.html` and `calculators/borrowing-power.html`
5. **Always link to a calculator when discussing affordability/repayments/refinance savings** — these are conversion-relevant, not just "more reading"
6. **Update this map** whenever `generate_lender_pages.py`, `generate_service_city_pages.py`, or `generate_topic_blogs.py` add new pages — also run `build_internal_links.py` per project convention
