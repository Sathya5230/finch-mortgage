# Finch Mortgages Style Guide

This guide defines writing conventions, formatting standards, and editorial guidelines for all Finch Mortgages content (blog, guides, service pages).

---

## Grammar & Mechanics

### Capitalization

**Headlines & Subheadings**: Title Case for H1s, Sentence case for H2/H3s (matches existing site pages, e.g. "First Home Buyer Mortgage NZ | 5% Deposit Loans | Finch")

**Product/Brand Names**:
- Finch Mortgages / Finch — always capitalized
- Mukhtar Kiyani — full name on first mention per page, "Mukhtar" thereafter
- Lender names use their own official capitalization: ANZ, ASB, BNZ, Kiwibank, Westpac, TSB, SBS Bank

**Industry Terms**:
- First Home Grant, Welcome Home Loan, KiwiSaver — proper nouns, always capitalized
- OCR, RBNZ, DTI, LVR, FSPR — acronyms, always caps
- mortgage broker / mortgage adviser — lowercase unless starting a sentence

### Numbers

**When to Spell Out**: Spell out one through nine in prose; use numerals for 10+, and always for:
- Percentages: 5%, 20% (never "five percent")
- Money: $500,000, $50 (never "five hundred thousand dollars")
- Rates: 6.49%, 1-year fixed, 2-year fixed
- Deposits/LVR: 20% deposit, 80% LVR

**Large Numbers**: Use commas ($500,000); spell out million/billion in prose ("$2 billion in loans settled")

### Punctuation

**Oxford Comma**: Yes (banks, lenders, and brokers)

**Em Dashes**: — (em dash, no spaces), used for parenthetical emphasis — matches existing site copy style

**Quotation Marks**: Straight quotes ("like this")

### Abbreviations & Acronyms

**First Use**: Spell out on first use with acronym following, e.g. "debt-to-income (DTI) ratio" — except universally known site-wide terms (OCR, RBNZ, NZ, SEO) which can be used unexplained after the first occurrence on a page

**Common NZ Mortgage Acronyms**:
- OCR: Official Cash Rate
- RBNZ: Reserve Bank of New Zealand
- DTI: Debt-to-Income (ratio)
- LVR: Loan-to-Value Ratio
- FSPR: Financial Service Providers Register
- FHOG / First Home Grant: Kāinga Ora first home buyer grant

**Latin Abbreviations**: Avoid "e.g."/"i.e." in body copy — use "for example" / "that is" in plain English; "etc." is fine sparingly

## Word Choice & Usage

### Preferred Terms

**Say This** → **Not That**
- home loan / mortgage → "loan product" (too vague)
- mortgage adviser / broker → "loan officer" (US term)
- deposit → "down payment" (US term)
- refinance (switching lender) / refix (new rate, same lender) → used interchangeably (these mean different things)
- pre-approval → "conditional approval" (unless quoting a specific lender's own term)
- First Home Grant → generic "government grant" (name it specifically)

### Words to Avoid
- Avoid jargon without explaining it on first use
- Avoid "very", "really", "simply", "just" unless load-bearing
- Avoid passive voice when active is clearer ("banks assess your DTI" not "your DTI is assessed by banks")
- Avoid "click here" — use descriptive link text
- Never invent or guess a specific rate, LVR/DTI threshold, or lender policy detail — verify against current site content or flag as needing confirmation

### Inclusive Language
- Gender-neutral language ("they" instead of "he/she") except when referring to Mukhtar Kiyani specifically
- Avoid idioms that assume a non-NZ context

## Formatting Standards

### Text Formatting

**Bold**: Key numbers, thresholds, and takeaways ("**20% deposit** avoids low-equity margins")

**Italics**: Sparingly, for emphasis or defined terms on first use

**ALL CAPS**: Avoid except standard acronyms (OCR, DTI, LVR, FSPR)

### Lists

**Bulleted Lists**: Used for eligibility criteria, lender comparisons, document checklists. Capitalize first word; period only if a complete sentence; keep parallel structure.

**Numbered Lists**: Used for sequential steps (e.g. the home-buying process, pre-approval steps)

### Links

**Anchor Text**: Descriptive, 2-6 words — "our first home buyer guide" not "click here"

**Internal Links**: Reference `context/internal-links-map.md` for the correct target page per topic

### Callout Boxes

**When to Use**: Key Takeaways blocks (see AI-search section of `seo-guidelines.md`), important caveats (e.g. "this is an estimate only")

## Content Structure

### Article Introduction (150-250 words)
1. **Direct answer** (1-2 sentences): Answer the core question immediately — NZ mortgage readers are often anxious and skimming for a specific number/answer
2. **Context** (2-3 sentences): Why this matters / who this applies to
3. **What's ahead**: Brief signpost of what the article covers

### Section Length
- 150-400 words per H2 section; break into H3s if longer

### Conclusion (100-200 words)
1. Recap key takeaways (bullets)
2. Clear next step: calculator tool, related guide, or "book a free consultation"

## SEO-Specific Style

### Meta Titles
- 50-60 characters, primary keyword near the start, "| Finch" or "| Finch Mortgages" if space allows
- Match existing pattern: "First Home Buyer Mortgage NZ | 5% Deposit Loans | Finch"

### Meta Descriptions
- 150-160 characters, includes primary keyword and a clear benefit/CTA
- Never invent a specific rate or approval claim in meta copy

### URL Slugs
- Lowercase, hyphenated, includes primary keyword, "-nz" suffix common on this site (matches existing URL patterns)

### Alt Text
- Describe what the image shows; 125 characters or less; no "image of"

## Dates & Time

**Date Format**: Month DD, YYYY (e.g. "July 6, 2026") in body copy; ISO format (YYYY-MM-DD) in structured data / dateModified fields, matching existing `datePublished`/`dateModified` schema on pages.

**Time Zones**: NZ time (NZST/NZDT) when relevant to rate-change timing.

---

## Statistics & Data

- Always cite the source (RBNZ, Stats NZ, REINZ, a named bank) for any statistic
- Include the year/date of the data
- Never present an estimate (e.g. a calculator output) as a guaranteed number — always caveat as an estimate subject to full lender assessment

---

## Compliance & Accuracy (Critical)

- **Never invent rates, LVR/DTI thresholds, grant amounts, or lender policy details.** If unverified, state that clearly or omit rather than guess.
- **Never claim a guaranteed approval, rate, or timeline** — mortgage advice is subject to individual circumstances and full lender assessment.
- Reference the FSPR registration (FSP1011206) and Financial Advice regulation only in already-established, accurate contexts (about/disclosure pages) — don't restate compliance claims casually in blog content.
