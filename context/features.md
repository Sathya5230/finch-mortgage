# Finch Mortgages Services & Differentiators

This document outlines Finch Mortgages' key services, benefits, and differentiators to inform content creation that drives consultation bookings and organic search visibility.

## Core Value Propositions

### 1. **Independent Lender Comparison**
- **Service**: Finch compares 20+ NZ lenders — major banks (ANZ, ASB, BNZ, Kiwibank, Westpac, TSB) and non-bank/specialist lenders (Resimac, Pepper Money, Liberty Financial, Avanti Finance, Heartland Bank, credit unions, and others) — rather than representing one bank.
- **Benefit**: The recommendation fits the client's situation, not one bank's current risk appetite.
- **Conversion Angle**: "We compare NZ's banks and non-bank lenders side by side, so you get the option that actually fits — not just what one bank is willing to offer this month."

### 2. **$0 Broker Fee**
- **Service**: Finch is paid by the lender on settlement, not by the client.
- **Benefit**: Independent mortgage advice at no direct cost to the borrower.
- **Conversion Angle**: "Expert, independent mortgage advice — at no cost to you."

### 3. **15+ Years of Main-Bank Experience**
- **Service**: Founder Mukhtar Kiyani spent 15+ years working inside ASB, Westpac, and BNZ before becoming an independent adviser.
- **Benefit**: Genuine insight into how banks assess applications, stress-test serviceability, and make credit decisions — not just how to fill out a form.
- **Conversion Angle**: "Get advice from someone who spent 15 years on the other side of the desk."

### 4. **FSPR-Licensed, Regulated Advice**
- **Service**: Registered on New Zealand's Financial Service Providers Register (FSP1011206), operating under the Financial Advice regime.
- **Benefit**: Advice carries a legal duty of care — not informal guidance.
- **Conversion Angle**: "Fully licensed, fully accountable financial advice."

### 5. **Fast Pre-Approval Turnaround**
- **Service**: Streamlined document collection and lender submission process.
- **Benefit**: Faster path to knowing your borrowing power before house-hunting or bidding at auction.
- **Conversion Angle**: "Know your numbers before you start looking — not after you've found the house."

### 6. **Specialist Support for Complex Cases**
- **Service**: Experience placing self-employed, low-deposit, previously-declined, and construction/new-build applications with the right lender.
- **Benefit**: Cases that a single bank might decline often still have a workable path through a different lender or structure.
- **Conversion Angle**: "A decline from one bank isn't the end of the road — we know which lenders say yes to cases like yours."

### 7. **Free Calculator Tools**
- **Service**: No-signup mortgage repayment, borrowing power, refinance savings, extra repayment, DTI, and rental yield calculators.

- **Conversion Angle**: "Get a real number in under a minute — no email required."

## Common Questions & Objections

### "Is using a mortgage broker actually free?"
**Answer**: Yes. Finch is paid a commission by the lender when your loan settles — there's no fee charged to you for standard residential lending.

### "Will a broker get me a better deal than going direct to my bank?"
**Answer**: A bank can only offer you its own products. Finch compares your situation against 20+ lenders' current policies and rates, so you see options a single bank never shows you — at no extra cost.

### "I was declined by my bank — can a broker still help?"
**Answer**: Often, yes. A decline usually reflects that specific bank's current risk settings, not an unfixable problem. We identify what triggered it and reposition the application, or place it with a lender whose policy fits.

### "How much deposit do I actually need?"
**Answer**: 20% gets the best rates and avoids low-equity margins, but many clients buy with 10%, and first home buyers can sometimes get in with 5% through specific lenders and schemes — see the First Home Buyer Guide for specifics.

### "How long does pre-approval take?"
**Answer**: It varies by lender and how complete your documentation is, but a well-prepared application can be pre-approved quickly — Finch manages the document collection to avoid delays.

## Content Creation Guidelines

When writing about Finch Mortgages services:

1. **Lead with the borrower's question, not the service name**: Don't just say "Refinance Mortgage" — open with "if your fixed rate is about to roll off, here's what to check."
2. **Use specific NZ mechanics**: KiwiSaver, First Home Grant, DTI, LVR, OCR — named correctly, not generic "government programs."
3. **Include proof points**: Mukhtar's 15+ years, FSPR registration, $0 fee — reinforce trust without overclaiming.
4. **Address objections proactively**: Cost, eligibility, and "will a bank actually say yes" are the three biggest objections — answer them before the reader has to ask.
5. **Create clear CTAs**: Calculator tool → guide → free consultation is the natural funnel.
6. **Never invent rates, lender terms, or approval odds**: All numeric claims (rates, LVR/DTI thresholds, grant amounts) must be verifiably accurate and current — do not fabricate or guess.
7. **Match audience to use case**: First home buyer content ≠ investor content ≠ self-employed content — keep the specific mechanics relevant to each segment.
