The NZ Mortgage Process:
Step by Step

6 min read Updated April 2026

Overview

The NZ mortgage process from first decision to settlement typically takes 6–10 weeks. With a Finch adviser by your side, each step is guided, documented, and progressed as quickly as the lender allows.

Step 1: Assess Your Financial Position

Before approaching any lender, understand your financial position. Key questions:

  • What is your gross annual income (and your partner's)?
  • What regular expenses do you have?
  • How much deposit do you have saved (including KiwiSaver)?
  • Any existing loans, credit cards, or financial commitments?

Finch does a full assessment at no cost, giving you a realistic picture of what you can borrow before you approach a lender.

Step 2: Get Pre-Approval

Pre-approval gives you a conditional commitment from a lender for a maximum borrowing amount. You'll need:

  • Last 2 years of payslips or tax returns (self-employed)
  • 3–6 months of bank statements
  • ID and proof of address
  • Details of your deposit and savings
Finch Tip: We apply to the lender most likely to approve your specific situation — not just the one with the lowest advertised rate. This maximises your approval odds.

Step 3: Search for a Property

Now that you know your budget, shop with confidence. Your pre-approval is typically valid for 90 days. During this period, you can bid at auction or make conditional offers on properties up to your pre-approved amount.

Step 4: Make an Offer

When you find a property, you'll sign a Sale and Purchase Agreement (S&P). For private sales, you can include conditions such as:

  • Subject to finance (3–5 working days)
  • Subject to building inspection
  • Subject to LIM report

At auctions, the sale is unconditional immediately — so you must have full pre-approval in place before bidding.

Step 5: Full Loan Approval

Once your offer is accepted, the lender will:

  • Order a registered property valuation
  • Confirm all your financial documents are in order
  • Issue a formal Letter of Offer

This usually takes 3–7 working days. Finch liaises with the lender daily to keep things moving.

Step 6: Legal Process

You'll need a conveyancer or property solicitor. They will:

  • Review the S&P agreement
  • Conduct title searches and LIM review
  • Prepare mortgage documents for signing
  • Coordinate settlement with the vendor's solicitor

Step 7: Settlement & Keys

On settlement day, your solicitor transfers the funds. The property title changes to your name. You collect the keys. You're a homeowner!

After settlement, Finch will set a reminder to review your loan before your fixed rate expires — ensuring you're always on the best deal available.

The Step-by-Step NZ Mortgage Journey

From first conversation to handing you the keys, a New Zealand mortgage application moves through eight distinct stages. Knowing what happens at each stage — and what your bank will need — lets you avoid the most common delays. This guide is the playbook Finch uses with every NZ client, whether you're in Auckland, Wellington, Christchurch, Hamilton, Tauranga, Dunedin, or anywhere else.

Stage 1: Discovery & Strategy Call

A free 15-minute discovery call where we map your income, deposit, KiwiSaver, expenses, and goal. We identify your borrowing window and the lenders most likely to favour your profile across the full panel of ANZ, ASB, BNZ, Westpac, Kiwibank, TSB, SBS, The Co-operative Bank, Heartland, and the specialist non-bank market.

Stage 2: Document Gathering

Standard NZ document checklist:

  • 3 months of payslips + employer letter (PAYE) OR 2 years of accountant-signed financials (self-employed)
  • 3 months of every bank statement including credit cards
  • KiwiSaver balance statement and annual summary
  • NZ photo ID + proof of address (AML compliance)
  • Evidence of deposit, regular savings history, and gift letters if applicable

Stage 3: Pre-Approval

We submit your file to the lender(s) most likely to win. Pre-approval is usually issued within 5–10 working days, conditional on a satisfactory property valuation, builders/LIM report where required, and the property being on the lender's acceptable list. Read more in our pre-approval service page.

Stage 4: House Hunting

With pre-approval in hand you can bid at auctions (the dominant sale method in Auckland, Wellington, Tauranga, Christchurch) or negotiate by private treaty in slower markets. We remain on call during your search for any property-specific questions about valuation outcomes or LVR adjustments.

Stage 5: Offer Accepted — Full Approval

Once your sale and purchase agreement is signed, your lender orders a valuation, finalises any outstanding conditions, and issues full unconditional approval — typically within 7–14 days.

Stage 6: Loan Documents & Solicitor Coordination

Your lender's solicitor sends loan and security documents to your solicitor. You sign and return; your solicitor lodges the mortgage instrument with LINZ via LandOnline.

Stage 7: Settlement Day

Funds clear from the lender to your solicitor's trust account, your solicitor pays the seller, and the title transfers. You receive the keys, normally the same afternoon.

Stage 8: Ongoing Mortgage Reviews

At every fixed-rate roll-off, and ideally annually, we review your loan structure, refinance for cashback where it makes sense, and recommend extra-repayment strategies. Use our extra repayment calculator regularly.
