How LVR works in New Zealand

Loan-to-value ratio is the size of your loan as a percentage of the property's value. A $640,000 loan on an $800,000 home is an LVR of 80%. The lower the LVR, the more of the property you own outright, and the less risk the lender carries.

The Reserve Bank's current LVR limits

The Reserve Bank of New Zealand limits how much high-LVR lending each bank can do. The current settings have applied since 1 December 2025, and the Reserve Bank kept them unchanged at its review in August 2026:

  • Owner-occupiers: up to 25% of a bank's new lending can be above 80% LVR, meaning a deposit under 20%.
  • Investors: up to 10% of a bank's new lending can be above 70% LVR, meaning a deposit under 30%.
  • Exemptions: new builds and Kāinga Ora First Home Loans are among the loans that sit outside these limits.

These are limits on banks, not on borrowers. A bank that has used up its allowance for the month may decline a low-deposit application that another bank would accept. Non-bank lenders aren't covered by the restrictions and set their own limits.

What a high LVR costs

Borrowers above 80% LVR usually pay more. Depending on the lender, that's a margin added to the interest rate or a one-off fee, and the size goes up as the deposit gets smaller. You're also less likely to be offered a bank's sharpest rates or a cashback. Our guide to the low equity margin explains how it's charged and how to get it removed.

Ways to lower your LVR

  • Add to the deposit with a KiwiSaver first-home withdrawal or a gifted deposit.
  • Use a family member's equity as extra security through a guarantee.
  • If you already own the property, pay down the loan or get an updated valuation once the value has risen.
  • Buy a new build, which is exempt from the Reserve Bank's limits.

LVR isn't the only test

A lender also checks that you can afford the repayments at a higher test rate, and that your total debt fits within the debt-to-income limits. To see how the three fit together, read what LVR means for a NZ mortgage or try the borrowing power calculator.

Common questions

How do I calculate LVR?

Divide the loan amount by the property's value and multiply by 100. A $640,000 loan on an $800,000 property is an LVR of 80%, which is the same as a 20% deposit.

What is the maximum LVR for a home loan in NZ?

There's no single maximum. The Reserve Bank lets banks make up to 25% of their new owner-occupier lending above 80% LVR and up to 10% of new investor lending above 70% LVR. New builds and Kāinga Ora First Home Loans are exempt, and each lender sets its own upper limit.

What LVR do I need to avoid a low equity margin?

Generally 80% or lower, which means a deposit or equity of at least 20%. Above that, most lenders add a margin to the rate or charge a fee.

Do LVR restrictions apply to new builds?

No. Loans to buy or build a new home are exempt from the Reserve Bank's LVR restrictions, although every lender still sets its own minimum deposit.
